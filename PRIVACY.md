## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Inboxed** ("we", "our", or "us"). Inboxed is a daily word puzzle game designed to be played directly inside your email inbox or on the web at [inboxed.fun](https://inboxed.fun).

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. We do not sell your personal data, we do not run third-party advertising tracking scripts, and we collect only the minimal data required to deliver the game and provide daily puzzles.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate and improve the game:

1. **Email Address:** When you subscribe, we collect your email address to send you daily puzzles, double opt-in confirmation emails, and account preference links.
2. **Game Activity & Progress:** When you play a puzzle, we record basic gameplay metrics such as the puzzle date, number of guesses, hints used, score, and whether the puzzle was solved. This information is stored securely in Cloudflare KV.
3. **Organization / Domain Information:** We derive your organization domain from your email address (for example, `company.com` from `player@company.com`) to calculate coworker leaderboard standings.
4. **Account & Privacy Preferences:** We store whether your subscription is active, paused, or unsubscribed, and whether you have enabled the Leaderboard Privacy toggle (which masks your display name as "Anonymous Player" on leaderboards).
5. **Technical Logs:** When you visit our website or play the game, standard, non-identifying server request logs (such as IP address, user agent, and timestamp) may be temporarily processed by Cloudflare for security, rate-limiting, and abuse prevention.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
- Delivering daily puzzle emails at 9:00 AM PST to confirmed subscribers.
- Verifying subscription requests via double opt-in confirmation emails.
- Calculating scores and org-level leaderboards.
- Honoring your account preferences, privacy settings, and unsubscribe requests.
- Protecting the service against abuse, automated bots, and rate-limit violations.

We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or trade your email address or personal data with any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 4. Email Communications & Anti-Spam Commitment

- **Daily Delivery:** Subscribers receive at most one puzzle email per day.
- **Double Opt-In:** We verify email addresses before activating daily delivery to ensure you requested the subscription.
- **Easy Unsubscribe:** Every email includes a standard one-click unsubscribe mechanism (`List-Unsubscribe` header) and a visible unsubscribe link in the email footer. You can unsubscribe immediately at any time without questions.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

We use reputable infrastructure partners to run our service:
- **Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers & Cloudflare KV):** Hosts our application code and securely stores game states and subscriber lists at the edge.
- **Mailgun:** Processes and delivers transactional confirmation and daily puzzle emails via secure SMTP.

These providers only process data as needed to perform their respective services on our behalf and are bound by their own rigorous security and privacy standards.

---

## 6. Security

- **Spoof-Proof Authentication:** We use cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 tokens for passwordless account and unsubscribe links, eliminating the need to store sensitive passwords.
- **Encryption:** All web traffic and API endpoints require HTTPS (TLS 1.3 encryption).

---

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

- **Leaderboard Anonymity:** You can toggle your display name between your email prefix and "Anonymous Player" at any time from your account settings.
- **Unsubscribe:** You can unsubscribe from daily emails at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or visiting `/account`.
- **Data Deletion:** You have the right to request complete deletion of your email address, gameplay history, and leaderboard records. To request deletion, email [game@inboxed.fun](mailto:game@inboxed.fun).

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Inboxed is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
